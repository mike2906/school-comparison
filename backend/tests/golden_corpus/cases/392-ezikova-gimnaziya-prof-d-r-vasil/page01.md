Admissions to Grades 5, 6 and 7 at Zlatarski International School of Sofia
Zlatarski International School of Sofia accepts applications to
grades 5, 6 and 7 for September 2026.
The School has been approved by Cambridge Assessment International Education to offer its world-leading Cambridge lower secondary programme for students aged 11-14.
Requirements
– Results from first term of current academic year – above 4,50
– Sitting an admissions exam
– Meeting between the candidates and their parents with the admissions committee
– Approval by the admissions committee according to the admission criteria (overall academic performance, results from the admissions exam, interview and motivation)

Registration for the exam:
Candidates register at the school by submitting an application form for sitting the exam, signed by the student and the parent. In addition, a copy of the student’s results are submitted – first page and page with marks from term one. The School provides sample exam papers at registration.
:
Open days are designed for candidates to Zlatarski International School and their parents. You will learn about specific aspects of the educational process at the school, the subjects in the Cambridge Secondary, IGCSE and IB Diploma Programme, extracurricular activities, preparation for world universities, requirements of the school of its students and the admissions criteria and dates. Students and parents will attend short taster sessions, will learn about the results of our students, will have the opportunity to talk to current students and teachers in different subjects, and they will have the opportunity to look through the textbooks and study guides we use at Zlatarski School.

Meetings with the Admissions Committee:
The School organises individual meetings with candidates and their parents. The aim is to establish the academic strengths and the motivation of students. Students applying to Zlatarski School
admit and share the values,
found in the School’s Mission; aim at achieving
high academic results;
expresses a willingness to
participate actively in school life
and aims to contribute to the School’s development as an international educational institution; shows readiness
to become a member of the international community
as a student at Zlatarski School. Meetings with the admissions committee are scheduled with the admissions office at registration.
Enrolment:
Enrolment takes place within the set deadlines at the School with:
– Document for completed year (gradebook or certificate for completed grade)
– 4 pictures (document format)
– medical card
– paid first installment and a signed contract for tuition, after agreeing with the School Rules and Procedures
From 1 September the School organises an English course for those students needing extra English classes.
