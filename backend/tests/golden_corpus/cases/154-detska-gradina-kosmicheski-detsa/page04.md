## SUMMER CAMPS
Sport surprises and surf adventures at Limnos Island for children:
* The sport lessons and the trainings include: windsurf, Hawaiian surf, paddle board, kayak
* Afternoons: swimming, diving, canoeing, sport games at the beach, in the water, beach tennis and beach volleyball, bikes and walks around the island.
