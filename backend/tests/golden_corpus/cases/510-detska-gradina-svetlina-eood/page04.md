Svetlina Educational Group was founded in 1999 by Nevena Radeva, a visionary who first introduced the holistic approach to education in Bulgaria. Every child is unique, and that is precisely why our team is trained to develop their potential – not only intellectually, but also physically, emotionally and spiritually.
Today, the Svetlina Educational Group is part of the Education 3000 global school chain, an international network that brings together the most successful innovative practices in the world. We are also an active member of the Association of Cambridge Schools in Bulgaria and the Bulgarian Association of Private Schools, sharing and applying high educational standards in our daily work.

Frequently Asked Questions
**Kindergarten**
### Do you have any vacancies?
Please contact our communication manager on 0890 000 009 or email kinder.communication.manager@svetlina.net to check for your desired location and group. If we do not have any spaces, we will put you on a waiting list and contact you as soon as possible.
### How big are the groups?
The groups are optimally 12 children. We have found that this is the optimal option, in which children have the opportunity to socialize, while at the same time receiving the necessary attention and individual approach.
### Do we get a point with you for applying to a state kindergarten?
You don’t get it.
### What are the working hours?
Our working hours start at 8:00 AM and end at 6:00 PM. An option for late pick-up of the child is available, which requires additional arrangement and additional payment.
### From what age do you accept children?
We do not have an age limit, but the child must be able to walk steadily.
### Do you work with catering?
Yes.
Children are fed four times a day:
Morning breakfast
Snack with fresh fruits and vegetables
Lunch with a three-course menu – soup, main course, dessert
Afternoon snack
The food is nutritious and fully complies with age needs and the standards of the Ministry of Health. Our goal is to provide children with a properly balanced and healthy diet, including plenty of seasonal fruits and vegetables. Early habituation to environmentally friendly eating, as well as good table manners, are also among our priorities.
We also offer an individual diet, tailored to the requirements of the family or pediatrician: for allergies, chronic diseases, and others.
### Do you have door to door transportation?
Private kindergarten “Svetlina” offers a specialized door-to-door children’s transport service. The transport is one-way or two-way. The service is paid additionally in addition to the tuition fee.
### Is an additional language being learned? What kind?
There is an opportunity to study an additional language, building on bilingual education.
**Private Innovative School**
### Why choose Svetlina CHOW?
As a holistic school, we offer education aimed at complex personal development with a focus on acquiring a set of skills that will help the student adapt to the rapid changes and dynamics in our living conditions. Our educational institution strives to provide protection, peace of mind and an adequate modern educational process with innovative methods both in-person and in digital learning.
Every day, we guide students towards conscious learning, independence in everyday life, teamwork and tolerance, emotional intelligence, physical and mental health, because the presence of the entire rich package of human virtues is very necessary in our time.
Our focus is on ensuring that the three equal parties to this process – parents, teachers and students – have a toolkit for interaction at all times and a variety of communication options. There are many individual differences and we take them into account, both in children and parents.
Our team consists of qualified educators, philologists, psychologists, sports instructors, music and art specialists who support and guide the student throughout his or her entire period of development and learning.
We believe that we cannot prepare the future for students, but we are ready to prepare students for their future!
