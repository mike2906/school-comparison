# Contact us
## Map & Directions
Our school campus is situated in the area of Detski Grad, near Pancharevo village. The best way to get to the campus is by car or taxi. Put the following address into the satellite navigation system to direct you: The Anglo-American School of Sofia For security purposes please book an appointment prior to visiting our school. All visitors will need to present ID at the security gate.
If arriving by taxi be sure to tell the operator that you want to go to **the Anglo-American School in Pancharevo**. Mentioning “Pancharevo” is important. Many taxi drivers don’t speak English. In case of difficulty call our receptionist on +359 2 923 8810 and they will help direct the driver.
