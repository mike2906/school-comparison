# Tuition Insurance
#### [Introducing Tuition Insurance at AAS Sofia
Peace of Mind for Your Family, Security for Your Child’s Education](https://www.aas-sofia.org/fs/resource-manager/view/c02a1bc0-7456-407b-8daa-f3526e7b2a05)
#### [Представяме Ви нов застрахователен продукт, който да допринесе за спокойствието на семейството Ви по отношение на таските за обучение на децата Ви](https://www.aas-sofia.org/fs/resource-manager/view/8fc495f3-a791-45f3-8d7d-28e11df6f542)
At the Anglo-American School of Sofia, we are committed to supporting your child’s education - even during life’s most difficult moments. That’s why we’ve introduced an exclusive **Tuition Insurance Policy** , created specifically for AAS families and provided by an independent, well-established insurance provider.
This innovative insurance plan helps ensure continuity in your child’s education by covering tuition fees in the event of serious, unforeseen circumstances affecting one or both parents.
##### What Does Tuition Insurance Cover?
The policy provides full coverage of tuition fees for one academic year if a parent faces any of the following life events:
* Loss of life
* Diagnosis of one of 15 severe critical illnesses
* Permanent disability
* Loss of working capacity for one or both parents
During such challenging times, Tuition Insurance offers both emotional and financial relief - ensuring your child’s education continues without interruption.
##### Key Features at a Glance
* **Insurance Amount:** Equal to the annual tuition fee
* **Tuition Payment Flexibility:** Tuition can still be paid in installments
* **Policy Validity:** One academic year
* **Insured Individuals:** One or both parents
* **Beneficiary:** The school or student, depending on the situation
* **Insurance Premium:**
2.7% of the tuition fee for one parent
3.7% for both parents
This exclusive offering reflects our school’s forward-thinking approach and commitment to student and family well-being.
