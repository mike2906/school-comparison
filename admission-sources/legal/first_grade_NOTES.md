## Groups by the leading criterion (чл. 9, т. 9.1–9.4)

Proximity to the school is the leading criterion. The school's прилежащ район (catchment area) is set by the council; for 2026/27 see § 2 of the amending ordinance. Children are placed in four groups:

1. **Group 1:**
   - Permanent or current address in the catchment area, **unchanged for more than 3 years** before the application.
   - Also **any child with a sibling aged up to 12** (age reached in the year of application) who is a pupil at the same school, whatever the address.
2. **Group 2:** address in the catchment area for more than 1 year, but changed within the last 1–3 years.
3. **Group 3:** address in the catchment area, but changed within the last year.
4. **Group 4:** address outside the catchment area.

Rules for applying the groups:
- If the address changed but stayed inside the same school's catchment area, the more favourable group applies (чл. 10).
- Adopted and foster children may use the adoptive or foster parent's address (чл. 13).
- Groups are filled in order. Within a group that is oversubscribed, children are ranked by extra points. Remaining ties go to a random number (чл. 7 ал. 6–7).
- Twins are placed together if one is admitted (чл. 7 ал. 8).
- Unlimited wishes; a child is placed in at most one school (чл. 7 ал. 3–4).

## Extra points (т. 9.5–9.12)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| 9.5 Disability, 50% or more | 6 |
| 9.6 One or both parents deceased | 6 |
| 9.7 Other children of the family studying at the same school | 4 |
| 9.8 Finished the preparatory group (ПГ) at the chosen school | 4 |
| 9.9 Family with more than two children | 5 |
| 9.10 Twins | 5 |
| 9.11 Foster or relative placement, unknown parent, or adopted | 3 |
| 9.12 Address in a catchment area **bordering** the school's | 5 |

- Only one of 9.9 and 9.10 can be used.
- 9.12 applies only to group 4, after groups 1–3 are exhausted.

## Address-choice lock (new чл. 6 ал. 5, чл. 11; Решение 201)

- The parent chooses **either** the child's permanent **or** current address. That address is used for all rankings of the school year.
- The address can be changed **only until the first ranking**.
- The address used in the first ranking is **kept for all later rankings**.
- Group placement uses the address chosen in the family profile (чл. 11).
- Note: address *duration* counts the **child's** address. Unlike the kindergarten ordinance, the parent's address plays no part.
