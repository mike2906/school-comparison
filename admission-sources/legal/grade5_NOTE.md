# 5th grade admission in Sofia: is there a common points system?

Retrieved 2026-10-14.

## Short answer

- **No municipal system.** Sofia has no municipal centralized points system for 5th grade comparable to the 1st-grade or kindergarten ordinances. Столична община's ordinances cover only nurseries, kindergartens, preparatory groups and 1st grade.
- **Ordinary schools.** Moving into 5th grade at an ordinary school is a *transfer* (преместване). It goes by **school-level criteria**: the school commission sets its own criteria when applications exceed free places, and time of application may not be a criterion (Наредба 10, чл. 45 ал. 2).
- **National exception.** There is one national, RUO-run **state plan for V grade**, only in **profiled gymnasiums with a "Математически" or "Природни науки" profile**. In Sofia these are, for example, СМГ, НПМГ and some others.

## The state plan for V grade (Наредба 10, чл. 73–74; text in `naredba10_art73_74_grade5.txt`)

**Who can apply:** pupils who completed 4th grade, sat the 4th-grade НВО, and took part in math or science competitions or olympiads from the MON calendar. They need either:
- one regional olympiad and one competition, or
- two competitions.

The competitions are chosen by the school's pedagogical council.

**Score (since the June 2025 amendment):**

    бал = НВО math (4th grade)
        + average of the 4th-grade grades converted to points
        + competition/olympiad results converted to % of the national top score,
          each weighted with a coefficient from 1 to 6 set by the pedagogical council

- The maximum is **950**.
- A pupil may form several scores and is ranked with the highest (чл. 74 ал. 4–5).

**Organisation:**
- RUO organises admission through a regional commission (чл. 73а).
- The minister sets the organisation by 31 March and the schedule before the school year.
