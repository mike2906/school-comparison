# Sofia kindergarten / nursery admission ordinance: notes

Retrieved 2026-10-14.

**Act:** Наредба за прием на деца в общинските самостоятелни детски ясли, детски градини и в подготвителните групи в общинските училища на територията на Столична община. It was adopted by Решение № 121 / 23.02.2023 and amended by **Решение № 200 / 12.03.2026** (Протокол № 58, точка 29), in force from 19.03.2026.

**Files:**

- `reshenie_200_2026.pdf` / `.txt`: the cover page of the council decision, from https://council.sofia.bg/documents/d/guest/r-200-2026. Its Приложение № 1, which holds the full text of the amending ordinance, was not found online.

Note: the ordinance title is no longer "...записване, отписване и преместване...". The criteria are in **чл. 18–20**, Раздел III.

## Points (чл. 18)

### Address (т. 1, т. 2)

**т. 1, address stability.** The address must be on Sofia territory and must not have changed as of the ranking date:
- more than 3 years: **8 pts**
- 1 to 3 years: **5 pts**
- under 1 year: **3 pts**

Whose address counts depends on the group:
- **Nursery groups (ясла) and 1st kindergarten group:** the permanent or current address of **one parent/guardian**, and it must **coincide with the child's** permanent or current address.
- **2nd to 4th groups and preparatory groups (ПГ) in schools:** the **child's** permanent or current address.

Permanent and current address are treated equally. A move *within* Столична община does not count as an address change.

**т. 2, address in the district.** 3 pts if the address is in the same administrative район as the kindergarten or school on the ranking date. It is the parent's address (who exercises parental rights) for nursery and 1st group, and the child's address for 2nd group onward and ПГ.

### Parents and siblings (т. 2а–8)

**Parent's employment or studies:**
- т. 2а / т. 3: working parent (employed, civil-service, or self-insured), or on child-care leave: **2 pts per parent**
- т. 4: parent who is a full-time pupil or student: **2 pts per parent**
- Only one of т. 3 or т. 4 can be used.

**т. 5, nursery point.** **1 pt** if the child attended a municipal or registered private nursery for at least 6 of the last 12 months. It applies only to applications for the 1st kindergarten group.

**Siblings and twins (only one of т. 6/7/8 applies):**
- т. 6: sibling attending the same institution at the same time: **1 pt**
- т. 7: twins: **1 pt**
- т. 8: siblings born less than 2 years apart: **1 pt**

### Social criteria (т. 9–15)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| т. 9: both parents deceased | 7 |
| т. 10: one parent deceased | 6 |
| т. 11: family member with 70% or more reduced work capacity | 4 |
| т. 12: unknown parent, parent with restricted or removed parental rights, or parent under interdiction or missing | 4 |
| т. 13: foster care, placement with relatives (чл. 26 ЗЗДт), or adopted | 3 |
| т. 14: child at risk | 3 |
| т. 15: family or household with 3 or more children | 2 |

For т. 13, the child may apply for groups 2–4 and ПГ with the address of the adoptive or foster parent if that is more favourable.

### Additional criteria (т. 16–17)

Only one of the two can be used:
- т. 16: SEN (СОП), up to 3 per group: **7 pts**
- т. 17: chronic illness, ТЕЛК 50% or more: **3 pts**

## Preference points and ties (чл. 19–20)

**Preference order (чл. 19):** 1st wish **+3**, 2nd **+2**, 3rd **+1**.
- These points drop by 1 when an already enrolled child applies for a transfer.
- They are kept if the parent declined a place by application.

**Ties (чл. 20):** resolved by random number.

**Outside the main rounds:** чл. 21 and 21а list cases admitted outside the rounds: children without parental care, abuse victims, children at risk, children of nursery or kindergarten staff, and children of a detained parent.

## Transitional provisions of the amending ordinance (Решение 200)

**§ 2, nursery point.** The т. 5 nursery point is **repealed from the date the free places for school year 2028/2029 are announced**.
- It therefore still applies in the 2026 and 2027 campaigns, for 2026/27 and 2027/28.
- The 1st kindergarten group admits children in the calendar year they turn 3. Under the enrollment-year rule, the 2027 campaign (for 2027/28) covers children **born 2024**.
- Children **born 2024 are the last birth cohort** that can get the nursery point in the regular 1st-group admission. Born 2025 (the 2028 campaign) will not get it.
- BTA (12.03.2026, https://www.bta.bg/bg/videos/35103) reports that the council *postponed the removal by one year, to 2028*. The municipality had proposed removing the point earlier. Deputy mayor Желязкова called it discriminatory.

**§ 3, new address text.** The new wording of чл. 18 т. 1 (one parent's address that coincides with the child's, and permanent equal to current) applies **from the announcement of free places for 2026/2027**. Before that date the old т. 1 text applied.
- The old text is not in the Ciela consolidated copy.
- Press coverage says the change is "equal weight of permanent and current address".

## Court status

See `court_appeals_NOTE.md`. АССГ suspended the address-stability provisions in April 2026. ВАС overturned that suspension on 07.05.2026 (final), so the rules applied in 2026.
