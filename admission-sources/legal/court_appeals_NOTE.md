# Court appeals against Решение 200 and Решение 201 of СОС (12.03.2026)

Retrieved 2026-10-14. All sources are press reports. Court acts were not obtained: the АССГ and ВАС sites were not searched case by case, and no case numbers were found for the first-instance cases.

## Решение 200: kindergartens and nurseries

**April 2026, suspension by АССГ.** Административен съд София-град suspended key provisions of the amended ordinance until the case is finally decided. The suspended provisions were the address-stability criterion and points, and the equal treatment of permanent and current address.
- https://paragraf.bg/sadat-sprya-chast-ot-novite-pravila-za-priem-v-detskite-gradini-v-sofiya/
- https://www.24chasa.bg/bulgaria/article/22682301
- https://dnes.dir.bg/obshtestvo/sadat-sprya-chast-ot-novite-pravila-za-priem-v-detskite-gradini-v-sofiya

**23.04.2026, municipality position.** The ISODZ criteria stayed unchanged pending ВАС's decision on the suspension: https://banker.bg/2026/04/23/kriterii-za-priem-isodz/

**07.05.2026, ВАС overturns the suspension.** Определение № 4913 / 07.05.2026, адм. дело № 4485/2026.
- ВАС upheld the private appeal by Столичен общински съвет, annulled the АССГ suspension and rejected the request to suspend.
- Reasoning: no proof of significant harm to the applicant; the lower court wrongly considered harm to an unlimited circle of people; public interest in equal treatment of permanent and current address.
- This ruling is **final**.
- **Effect:** the new rules applied to the 2026/27 campaign.
- Source: https://www.bta.bg/bg/news/bulgaria/oficial-messages/1120999-vas-otmeni-opredelenieto-na-assg-za-spirane-deystvieto-na-chast-ot-novite-pravil
- Some outlets (novini247, actualno) described this as ВАС "confirming the rules are lawful". It was only a ruling on the suspension, not on the merits.

**Merits.** No first-instance ruling on the merits was found as of 14.10.2026. The status is **pending or unknown**.

## Решение 201: 1st grade

**26.03.2026.** An АССГ news item is headed "Административен съд София-град спира част от промените в Наредбата за прием в първи клас" (https://sofia-adms-g.justice.bg/bg/news1). No details were readable. It may refer to the kindergarten case or to an early 1st-grade suspension request; this is unverified.

**27–28.05.2026, АССГ ruling against amendments of Решение 201.**
- clubz.bg says the court "спрял действието на § 3 и § 5 от измененията... приети с решение № 201".
- BTA video (28.05.2026) and others describe the amendments as "annulled" ("отменя") at first instance.
- The contested rule is the address choice (permanent or current) and its lock after the first ranking (чл. 6 ал. 5).
- The ruling is **not final**. Sofia Municipality announced an appeal to ВАС.
- Deputy mayor Желязкова said the March rules stay in force until the final decision, which is expected after the 2026 campaign. The 02.06.2026 ranking went ahead under the March rules.
