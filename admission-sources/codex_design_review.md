# Codex design review: admission calculator

Date: 2026-10-14. Codex CLI 0.162.1 (`codex exec -s read-only`), run on agent/admission-rules @ 2fa69e9 with admission-sources/ copied in. Links point to that local checkout.

The answer below is verbatim.

---

The evaluator approach can survive expansion. The current versioning and boundary handling cannot yet support a trustworthy parent-facing calculator.

Top issues, in priority order:

1. **Revisions do not actually version the calculation.** [Design:26](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/docs/ADMISSION_POINTS.md:26) has several revisions but only one `params` object; [core.js:121](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/frontend/src/admission/core.js:121) selects revision metadata without selecting its parameters. Historical calculations therefore cannot be reproduced. More seriously, adoption/in-force dates are insufficient: [transitional provisions:14](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/admission-sources/legal/reshenie_200_PZR.txt:14) repeal the nursery point **from announcement of 2028/29 places**, while §3 activates the new address wording **from announcement of 2026/27 places**. These are separate applicability triggers within the same act. A suspension also needs an explicit calculation consequence; returning an event alongside unchanged parameters does not resolve it.

2. **“Never roll forward” currently rolls forward with a warning.** [core.js:115](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/frontend/src/admission/core.js:115) returns earlier rules for an unconfigured campaign. That can award the nursery point in 2028/29 despite its already enacted repeal. Conversely, an exact campaign match is automatically `confirmed`, irrespective of verification or unresolved applicability. Separate “verified applicable rules” from “illustration using an older campaign”; a warning cannot make an obsolete calculation correct.

3. **The kindergarten presentation confuses score components with admission routes.** [Design:53](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/docs/ADMISSION_POINTS.md:53) describes general-queue, social-queue and additional points. Summing applicable general + social + additional + wish points is a defensible reading of чл.18–20, respecting exclusions. But the total does **not** describe priority or capacity:
   - [чл.14(5)](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/admission-sources/legal/kg_ordinance.txt:87): social priority applies to the **first ranking for new-year intake**, up to 30% of announced places. This is not a 30% score adjustment or sufficient evidence of a separate social-only score.
   - Чл.14(6): **two SEN places announced**, with a possible third referral; exceptions exist.
   - Чл.14(9): chronic-illness admission is capped at three per group; that alone does not establish three reserved vacancies.
   
   Show one points breakdown plus separate eligibility/priority explanations. Verify operational allocation and rounding before interpreting historical cut-offs by route.

4. **First-grade duration cannot reuse the kindergarten tiers unchanged.** [First-grade text:138](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/admission-sources/legal/first_grade_system.txt:138) says group 1 requires **more than three years**: exactly three belongs in group 2. Group 2 expressly requires **more than one year**, so exactly one cannot be assigned there using `at_least_years: 1` as in [core.test.js:51](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/frontend/src/admission/core.test.js:51). That test is suitable for kindergarten, not first grade. Also measure at **application**, not kindergarten’s ranking date; preserve same-catchment address history under чл.10, the sibling override and adoptive/foster exceptions. Under [чл.6(5)](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/admission-sources/legal/first_grade_system.txt:61), the chosen address is locked across schools and later rankings—do not independently select each school’s most favourable address.

5. **The court notes are incomplete enough to mis-version rules.** The uncertainty at [court notes:26](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/admission-sources/legal/court_appeals_NOTE.md:26) can partly be resolved: **ВАС Определение №5326, 18.05.2026, дело №4277/2026** overturned the **25 March** suspension of first-grade §3/§5, identifies the challenged wording, and is final regarding suspension. It does **not** settle the merits or resolve the reported later May judgment. Record those proceedings separately; “appealed”, “suspended” and “annulled” cannot be interchangeable statuses. [Primary court act](https://info-adc.justice.bg/courts/portal/edis.nsf/e_act.xsp?code=vas&guid=1274720299&id=2636546).

6. **JSON plus evaluators holds up; “every Bulgarian city is just parameters” is overpromised.** [Design:16](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/docs/ADMISSION_POINTS.md:16) conflates the national grouping template with municipal address-selection and application procedures. Share the national grouping calculation; allow explicit municipal policy code when behaviour differs. Other countries also need their own campaign conventions and result shapes, not necessarily September starts, points or four groups. There is already a concrete scope bug: [core.js:111](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/frontend/src/admission/core.js:111) cannot select national grade-eight rules when the request includes `city: 'sofia'`.

7. **The catchment approach is sound, but lookup identity and dataset versioning are underspecified.** [Catchment README:16](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/admission-sources/catchments/README.md:16) requires settlement + street identity, number and potentially entrance—not simply street and number. The directed neighbouring-area calculation is correct. Use the address table for classification and simplified polygons for display; snapshot both by campaign rather than overwrite historical geometry. GIS IDs are public, but a bare universal `catchment_id` creates the wrong contract: namespace it by provider/jurisdiction. Also [the adapter:579](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/backend/app/scrapers/sources/bg/kg_sofia.py:579) preserves multiple ESRI IDs for merged buildings, so projecting only the singular attribute can lose associations. Prefer the supplied district over inferred coordinates where available.

8. **Passing tests conceal invalid-input and privacy gaps.** I reproduced these in [core.js:47](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/frontend/src/admission/core.js:47): impossible dates are accepted; missing measurement dates and future registration dates produce an exact floor score; empty tiers produce `{min: Infinity, max: -Infinity}`. Leap-day anniversary policy is undefined. Ranges also need evaluator-level constraints: summing independently uncertain, mutually exclusive answers can produce unattainable maxima. [grade8.js:10](/home/mike2906/sofiaschoolcomparison/.claude/worktrees/admission-review-wt/frontend/src/admission/evaluators/bg/grade8.js:10) silently omits additional НВО subjects and olympiad components permitted by чл.57, returns zero for missing formulas/invalid grades, and accepts out-of-range scores. Browser-only answers are the right privacy default, but route-loaded code alone provides no isolation from analytics, error capture or other scripts; address-driven map tiles can disclose approximate location.

My top three recommendations:

1. **Make resolved rule versions immutable:** each selects actual parameters and evaluator version, with explicit campaign/event applicability and a separate verified status. Historical cut-offs must identify the resolved calculation.
2. **Keep explicit evaluators, loosen the universal promises:** share arithmetic and national grouping logic; allow municipal policy differences. Return score/group, eligibility and priority as distinct results.
3. **Require boundary and privacy evidence before launch:** add adversarial tests for dates, exact anniversaries, address locks, exclusions, missing formulas and unsupported grade-eight cases; verify that entered answers and addresses never reach network requests or telemetry.

The existing core tests pass; the reproduced failures are outside their coverage.
